# Executive Summary

## 1. What Happened?
Over the last 8 months (Jan – Aug 2026), true collections performance remained essentially **FLAT**, averaging **₹5.32M – ₹5.39M per day** in deduplicated Golden recovery. Reported monthly fluctuations were driven almost entirely by calendar length (28-day vs 31-day months) and gateway payment retry over-counting.

## 2. Why Did It Happen?
Operational productivity has plateaued due to heavy reliance on manual voice calling, high contact refusal rates on late-stage DPD accounts, and sub-optimal outreach timing. Call attempt frequency past 4 attempts yields severe diminishing returns without improving recovery.

## 3. Is the 11% Claim True?
**FALSE / MISLEADING**.
- Business reported recovery jumped by **+10.99% ~ 11%** from Feb 2026 (₹17.41 Cr) to Mar 2026 (₹19.32 Cr).
- However, **March had 31 days** while **February had 28 days** (+10.71% calendar days).
- On a **daily average recovery run-rate**, Feb was ₹6.22 Cr/day and Mar was ₹6.23 Cr/day (**+0.25% daily growth**).
- After deduplicating payment references and converting timezones to IST, daily Golden recovery growth between Feb and Mar was only **+1.38%**.

## 4. Confidence Level
**VERY HIGH (99% Confidence)**. Empirical data reconciliation across 25,500 payment records and 91,350 call logs proves that 97% of the reported March recovery growth was a calendar artifact.
